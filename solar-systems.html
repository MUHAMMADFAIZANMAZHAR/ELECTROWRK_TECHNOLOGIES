System Topologies

Compare Solar System Topologies

Every home and commercial facility in Pakistan has unique power priorities and grid reliability conditions.

1. On-Grid Solar System

Directly synchronized with the utility grid (LESCO, IESCO, FESCO, K-Electric). Features bi-directional green Net-Metering to export excess units back to the grid and reduce monthly utility bills to near zero.

  • Highest Return on Investment (ROI in 2.2 - 2.8 Years)
  • Zero battery cost or replacement overhead
  • Net-Metering export credits on electricity bills
  • Ideal for homes, commercial plazas, and factories
₨ 450,000+ Starting Turnkey Price

2. Off-Grid Solar System

100% independent standalone solar power plant equipped with heavy-duty battery storage. Operates completely detached from the grid, generating, storing, and supplying clean energy day and night.

  • Complete freedom from grid power and tariff hikes
  • Deep cycle Tubular or Lithium (LiFePO4) storage
  • Uninterrupted 24/7 power in remote locations
  • Suitable for farmhouses, agricultural sites & villas
₨ 350,000+ Starting Turnkey Price

3. Hybrid Solar System

The ultimate dual-mode solar system. Combines daytime grid-tied savings and Net-Metering exports with instant battery backup failover during load shedding and power outages.

  • Net-Metering bill reduction + Zero blackout backup
  • Automatic instantaneous power failover (< 10ms)
  • Smart load priority (Solar → Battery → Grid)
  • Best choice for residential villas in Pakistan
₨ 650,000+ Starting Turnkey Price

Site Feasibility

02

Application & SLD

03

Inspection & NOC

04

Meter Activation
